The Night

The Asheville Beer Choir turned back the clock for one glorious evening of Songs from 1985 — a prom-themed concert where the sequins sparkled, the sheet music got waved like pom-poms, and every chorus landed like a slow-dance under a disco ball.

Here's the whole night — the full set list below, each song linked to the moment it happened in the concert video, and a gallery of frames from the floor.
